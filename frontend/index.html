About Mary

Experience:

Mary is a licensed massage therapist with 39 years of experience in therapeutic and medical massage. As a breast cancer survivor, she has become passionate about lymphatic and oncology massage.


Her extensive clinical background allows her to work with clients managing post-surgical recovery, chronic conditions, and medically complex needs, while maintaining a high standard of safety and professional care.
